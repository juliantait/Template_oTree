The payoff matrix

The table below shows the points each player earns for every combination of choices.

Reminder: you are the row player. Your payoff is the first number in the cell selected by your row and your partner's column.

  • If both of you choose Stag, you each earn 4 points.
  • If both of you choose Hare, you each earn 2 points.
  • If you choose Stag but your partner chooses Hare, the Stag-chooser earns 0 and the Hare-chooser earns 2.
Remember: Stag pays 4 points only when both of you choose it. Hare always pays 2.
