{# TWO WORDINGS, BECAUSE ROUND 2 IS A DIFFERENT SITUATION (Julian, 2026-08-13, change_requests_round2 item 2). Round 2 of `intro` is the lab re-read pass, and it is reachable ONLY by a participant who already failed the quiz and took the one-time re-read offer (intro.in_reread_pass). So by construction their `failed_attempts` is non-zero — and the quiz bonus is paid only when `failed_attempts == 0` (outro/__init__.py, compute_final_payoff). The round-1 sentence therefore states a condition that CANNOT be met any more, and worse, "on the first attempt" implies the second pass restores a first attempt. It does not. Round 2 says only what is still true: there is a quiz next, and you may reread first. NO bonus, NO first attempt. Round 1 is unchanged apart from bolding the amount (item 3). `is_reread_pass` comes from instructing.vars_for_template — a server-side value, not a round number typed into the template, so the two situations stay distinguishable at exactly one place. #}
