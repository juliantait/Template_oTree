Your final payment will be the sum of your earnings from the rounds, plus a show-up fee of {{ payment_show_up }} and a bonus of {{ payment_quiz_bonus }} if you answer every quiz question on the instructions correctly on your first attempt.

The game

In each round you and your partner independently choose one of two actions: Stag or Hare. You make your choice without knowing what your partner picked.

Hunting a stag is only successful if both of you go for it. Hunting a hare always succeeds on your own, but the catch is smaller.

Your role

{% if treatment == "row" %}

You have been assigned the role of row player for the entire experiment. On the payoff matrix on the next page, your choice selects the row, and your partner's choice selects the column.

In each cell of the matrix the two numbers are written as (your payoff, partner's payoff). Your earnings are always the first number in the cell.

{% elif treatment == "column" %}

You have been assigned the role of column player for the entire experiment. On the payoff matrix on the next page, your choice selects the column, and your partner's choice selects the row.

In each cell of the matrix the two numbers are written as (partner's payoff, your payoff). Your earnings are always the second number in the cell.

{% endif %}

What this means in practice

  • If both of you choose Stag, you each earn {{ stag_payoff }} points.
  • If both of you choose Hare, you each earn {{ hare_payoff }} points.
  • If you choose Stag but your partner chooses Hare, the Stag-chooser earns {{ stag_alone }} and the Hare-chooser earns {{ hare_payoff }}.

So Stag pays more if you coordinate, but Hare is a safer choice that pays the same regardless of what your partner does.

Remember: Stag pays {{ stag_payoff }} points only when both of you choose it. Hare always pays {{ hare_payoff }}.
